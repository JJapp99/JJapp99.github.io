Research

My research sits between exercise physiology and behavioral science. I study exercise that is short and simple enough to do at home, how to prescribe its intensity for each person, and what helps people keep doing it.

Projects

ISO-BP: Individualized Home-Based Isometric Wall Squat Training for Blood Pressure

Ongoing RCTExercise Physiology Laboratory, Ewha Womans University × Division of Cardiology, Korea University Guro Hospital

A randomized controlled trial testing home-based isometric wall squat training in adults with hypertension, with exercise intensity prescribed individually rather than by a fixed protocol.

  • Developed the participant-facing mobile app for timer-based exercise delivery, RPE and heart rate logging, and adherence monitoring
  • 24-hour blood pressure phenotyping (nocturnal dipping, BP variability) with a ring-type continuous BP device (CART-BP)
  • Study protocol under review at BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation

Antihypertensive Effects of Isometric Exercise Snacks

M.S. thesisPrincipal Investigator · National Research Foundation of Korea Master's Student Research Grant, 2026–2027

A CART-BP-based randomized controlled trial asking whether brief bouts of isometric exercise spread across the day (“exercise snacks”) can lower blood pressure, as a format that is easier to start and to sustain than a conventional session.

Physical function
SPPB (AndanteFit), 6-minute walk test, grip strength, gait speed, 5× sit-to-stand, goniometry
Data & tools
SPSS, R, CRF and data schema design, mobile app development for exercise delivery and adherence monitoring
